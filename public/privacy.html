RideVector

Privacy

Updated November 11, 2026

Your private cycling workspace

RideVector uses the information you provide to display your cycling history, calculate personal trends, and support your training and race planning. Your saved dashboard is accessible through your own signed-in account. Strava and other optional connections are not required to use RideVector.

Information we handle

We handle your account email and authentication information; ride titles, times, distance, elevation and available GPS routes, heart rate and power recordings; and the goals, races, training plans, notes, zones and coaching preferences you choose to save. GPS tracks can reveal precise locations. Upload only recordings you intend to store in your account.

When you authorize a supported activity provider, RideVector uses the granted access to import your activities. Provider access and refresh tokens are stored on the server rather than exposed in the browser. Garmin and Wahoo connections are subject to provider approval and are not available until enabled.

Files and Apple Health

Supported activity files are parsed in your browser. RideVector stores the extracted ride information and available recordings, rather than the original uploaded file. Identical files are recognized by a file hash.

The iPhone companion, when installed and enabled, requests read-only access to cycling workouts and available distance, workout routes, heart rate and cycling power in Apple Health. It imports the last 90 days only when you tap Import, into the RideVector account signed in on that device. It does not write to Apple Health. You can change Health permissions in the Health app. Revoking permission stops future reads but does not remove previously imported RideVector records. The companion is under development and is not yet distributed through the App Store.

How information is processed

Vercel hosts the web application and server functions. Supabase provides authentication and stores private account and ride records in Postgres with ownership-based access controls. Training suggestions and charts use deterministic calculations in your browser. RideVector does not send ride, health, race or coaching data to an external AI service, sell it, or use it for targeted advertising.

OpenStreetMap supplies map tiles for the area you view; route coordinates are drawn locally. Loading those tiles can reveal the viewed area and your network address to the tile provider. Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and application libraries and receive normal web requests, including your network address. Hosting providers may process operational logs needed to run and secure the service.

Storage and optional sharing

RideVector stores authentication session information in your browser to keep you signed in. Theme preferences are stored on your device. Optional calendar subscription links allow a calendar client holding the link to read saved training and race titles, dates, target durations and completion status. Keep that link private and revoke it in Calendar sync settings when no longer needed. Calendar clients control their own refresh timing and cached copies.

Your controls and retention

You can remove ride records in your dashboard and disconnect Strava in Connections. Disconnecting Strava removes its imported records; separately uploaded files and manually entered rides remain. Matching recordings from different sources may count once while their original source records remain saved. Removing one source copy does not necessarily remove another copy.

Saved information remains in your account until removed or the account is deleted. Contact us to request account deletion, a copy of your stored information, or help removing data. Service-provider backups and operational logs may retain information according to those providers’ retention processes after deletion from the active application. Revoking a connection does not delete information held by the original activity provider.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact RideVector at thetylerjp@icloud.com. Please send your request from the account email where possible; we may need to verify account ownership.
