What’s in this update 9 changes
New feature

Log the minutes that matter

Choose Indoor cycling when adding a ride. Duration is required; distance and elevation are optional. Add available heart rate, power, cadence averages, and notes. Missing distance stays unknown and is never estimated.

Improvement

Indoor rides from your favorite tools

Strava trainer and virtual cycling rides and Wahoo indoor, spin-class, stationary-bike, trainer, and virtual sessions can import without distance. Garmin indoor cycling is supported through exported FIT or TCX files; direct Garmin syncing still awaits provider approval. Reimport older files or run provider imports again to recognize indoor metadata.

Improvement

Time counts across your workspace

Indoor sessions contribute to ride counts, riding time, calendar history, duration-based training trends, and time records. Mileage uses recorded distances only. Indoor details lead with duration and available sensors, and omit unavailable maps and distance charts. Sensor cards and single charts expand to fit the available space.

Improvement

Careful matching without mileage

Time-only duplicate matching requires explicit indoor classification, different sources, sessions of at least five minutes, starts within five seconds, and durations within 1% or five seconds. Manual entries, conflicting groups, and uncertain matches stay separate; original source copies remain available.

New feature

Try an indoor spin without signing in

The release playground includes a sample 45-minute indoor ride. Watch riding time and ride count increase while distance stays unchanged. The sample stays in temporary browser memory and does not change your account.

Improvement

See every indoor minute

Time in the saddle and weekly recaps now show hours and minutes instead of decimal hours. Indoor/outdoor breakdowns appear on the dashboard, in weekly recaps, and in the race coach. The coach explains how indoor time counts and distinguishes its Monday–Sunday week from the selected dashboard range. Whole-minute breakdowns add up to the displayed total; training calculations keep the original recorded seconds.

New feature

Android has a home here

The installation guide now includes Android Chrome setup, alongside the existing iPhone Safari steps. Launch RideVector from your Home Screen or app launcher in its own window, with no app store download.

Improvement

A smoother invitation to install

When the browser offers installation, use Install RideVector directly. The button prevents repeated prompts, handles dismissal and errors, and confirms completed installation. Browser-menu instructions remain available even when no prompt appears.

Improvement

The same riding workspace

Android uses the existing phone navigation, themed dashboard, controlled updates, and public offline guides. Sign-in, private rides, saving, and provider imports still need a connection; installation does not add health access or background tracking.

What’s in this update 17 changes
New feature

Connect with Wahoo

Authorize read-only access to your Wahoo profile and workouts in Connections. Import completed cycling workouts directly, including distance, climbing, active timer duration, and available sensor averages.

Improvement

Make room for your history

Import up to 30 workouts per request, then continue with Import older rides. Reimports update the same source recording; matching Strava and file recordings count once. Wahoo does not return workouts originating in third-party apps.

Improvement

Your files still come along

Upload original FIT files for route maps and sensor graphs, or for history unavailable through Wahoo. Wahoo imports run when you press Import; background syncing is not enabled. Disconnect revokes Wahoo access and retains previously imported rides, which you can remove individually.

Improvement

A clearer import result

Wahoo imports check individual cycling workouts when the list omits completed results. Import messages distinguish entries without completed results, non-cycling workouts, and missing ride fields.

Improvement

One day, one update

Same-day releases now share a single card. Search, filters, sharing, older direct links, and interactive previews stay available.

New feature

Take the features for a spin

Try sample Wahoo imports and duplicate grouping without signing in. Preview your own FIT, GPX, TCX, or CSV files locally in a temporary workspace; nothing is uploaded or saved.

New feature

RideVector on your Home Screen

Install the web app from Safari on iPhone with RideVector icons and its own app window. The new installation guide walks through setup; browsers that support a native install prompt can use it too.

Improvement

Made for a phone in your hand

Bottom navigation, a More menu, larger form controls, and safe-area spacing bring the dashboard closer to a phone app. Your current section survives a reload, and provider callbacks return to Connections.

New feature

A clearer connection to the road

Public guides, release notes, and their playground can load from the offline cache after setup. Private dashboard data, authenticated responses, and provider APIs are never cached by the service worker. Sign-in, saving, and imports need a connection.

Improvement

Updates at your pace

A new build offers Reload & update instead of automatically refreshing your workspace. Open dialogs and active ride imports block that reload until you finish. Apple Health remains part of the separate native companion in development. Each build refreshes the public offline cache.

New feature

Your release journal

Explore shipped features, improvements, and fixes in one place, with links from the dashboard.

Improvement

Find your next discovery

Search updates, filter by change type, expand the details, or copy a direct link to a release. Interactive examples let you explore selected features with sample data.

Improvement

A living record

Release notes are now part of RideVector’s update workflow, with a repository check that flags product changes missing a release-note update.

New feature

FIT files, welcome aboard

Import recorded cycling sessions with available GPS, heart-rate, and power data. Device timer duration is shown separately from elapsed and moving time.

New feature

Bring the whole riding story

Select up to 100 FIT, GPX, TCX, or CSV files in one batch: up to 10 MB per file, 50 MB combined, and 2,000 rides. Individual file failures are reported so you can retry.

Improvement

One ride, counted once

Confidently matched recordings from different sources count once while original source copies stay saved. Manual entries and uncertain matches stay separate. Manage the grouping in Connections.

Improvement

Strava is optional

Continue without Strava is now prominent in onboarding. A RideVector account, your activity files, or manual ride entry are enough to get started.

New feature

Your plans, in Apple Calendar

Create a private, revocable calendar subscription for saved training sessions and races. It’s a one-way feed; your calendar app controls refresh timing.

Improvement

Time comes first

Coach drafts use recent riding minutes, consistency, recovery check-ins, and usable cached power or heart-rate evidence. Distance stays a secondary estimate.

Improvement

Room for the week you actually had

Future, unedited adaptive coach sessions can adjust as RideVector refreshes, imports rides, or receives a check-in. Personal edits and completed or past sessions stay protected. Missed riding isn’t turned into catch-up work.

Fix

Clearer coaching context

Coach wording now distinguishes adaptive sessions from personal plans and handles stale account loads more carefully. Training suggestions remain transparent planning heuristics, not readiness predictions.
