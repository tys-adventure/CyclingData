Fitness vs. Fatigue

Daily training trends

Duration-based estimate
Fitness · solid lineFatigue · dashed line

What is Fitness?

Your longer-term riding trend. It builds gradually with consistent riding and eases down during breaks. RideVector estimates it from a 42-day response to your recorded ride minutes.

What is Fatigue?

Your recent riding workload. It responds faster to busy riding days and falls faster during rest. RideVector estimates it from a 7-day response to your recorded ride minutes.

When Fatigue is above Fitness, your recent riding load is higher than your longer-term trend. These duration-based estimates do not measure how tired you feel, your power, or your race readiness.

How these estimates work

Recorded ride minutes feed two daily exponential averages: a 42-day Fitness response and a 7-day Fatigue response. Both use the same min/day scale and include rest days. Every range uses all earlier recorded rides, so changing the range does not reset the calculation. These are training-volume estimates, not intensity-based TSS or Strava Fitness scores. Ride duration uses the time available from each import source; Strava uses moving time. History before your first recorded ride is assumed zero, and today's value includes only rides already recorded.

Apple Calendar subscription

Create a private link for workout and race titles, dates, target minutes, and completion status. Anyone with this link can read those calendar details. Keep it private.

This is a one-way subscription. Your Apple Calendar refreshes it periodically; edits in Apple Calendar do not update RideVector. Recorded rides, GPS, sensor data, and personal notes are excluded.

Subscribe in Apple Calendar

On Mac: Calendar → File → New Calendar Subscription. Choose an auto-refresh interval. On iPhone: add a subscribed calendar using this URL. Choose iCloud where available to use it across devices.

Replacing or disabling the link stops future access through the old URL. Remove an unwanted subscription from Apple Calendar to clear its cached events.
