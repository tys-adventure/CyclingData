Open ridevector.app/install in Chrome on your Android phone or tablet. If you arrived inside another app, open this page in Chrome first.

Using an iPhone? Safari steps ↓
  1. 01

    Open Chrome’s menu.

    Tap the three dots (⋮) beside the address bar. If an Install RideVector button appears above, you can use that instead.

  2. 02

    Choose Install.

    Look for Install and create shortcut → Install. Depending on your Chrome version, this may be labeled Add to Home screen or Install app. Follow the browser’s confirmation steps.

  3. 03

    Launch from your apps.

    Find the RideVector icon on your Home Screen or in your app launcher. It opens in its own app window. Sign in if prompted, and you’re ready to ride.

IPHONE · SAFARI

Three little steps.

Use Safari on your iPhone. If you’re in another app’s browser, open ridevector.app/install in Safari first.

  1. 01

    Open the sharing menu.

    In Safari, tap Share. Depending on your iOS version, it may be inside the More menu.

  2. 02

    Choose Add to Home Screen.

    Scroll through the sharing actions to find it. If it’s missing, choose Edit Actions to add it.

  3. 03

    Keep Open as Web App enabled.

    If that switch appears, leave it on. Tap Add, then launch the RideVector icon from your Home Screen.

A few useful details.

How do Wahoo and Strava connections work?

Start from Connections inside the installed app. Authorize on the provider’s site and return to RideVector. If authorization returns to a browser window instead, you may need to sign in there; reopen the Home Screen app and refresh Connections to see the saved server-side connection. Keep the whole authorization flow in the browser window it starts in.

Can I import ride files from my phone?

Yes. Choose Add a ride, then select FIT, GPX, TCX, or CSV files through your phone’s file picker. Saving imported rides requires a connection.

Does this connect to Apple Health?

This web app does not read Apple Health. The separate iPhone companion is still in development and needs signing and real-device testing.

Why don’t I see an Install button?

The browser decides when to offer installation, so the button may not appear immediately or if RideVector is already installed. Use Chrome’s menu on Android or Safari’s sharing menu on iPhone. Other browsers, including Samsung Internet, may offer installation in their own menus; labels vary. You can always keep using ridevector.app in your browser.

Does Android need Google Play or special permissions?

No Google Play download is needed. Installation itself does not grant access to health data, background ride tracking, or push notifications. RideVector’s existing sign-in and provider connections work over the web, and private rides, saving, and syncing need a connection.
