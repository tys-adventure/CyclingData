MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

Three little steps.

Use Safari on your iPhone. If you’re in another app’s browser, open ridevector.app/install in Safari first.

  1. 01

    Open the sharing menu.

    In Safari, tap Share. Depending on your iOS version, it may be inside the More menu.

  2. 02

    Choose Add to Home Screen.

    Scroll through the sharing actions to find it. If it’s missing, choose Edit Actions to add it.

  3. 03

    Keep Open as Web App enabled.

    If that switch appears, leave it on. Tap Add, then launch the RideVector icon from your Home Screen.

A few useful details.

How do Wahoo and Strava connections work?

Start from Connections inside the installed app. Authorize on the provider’s site and return to RideVector. If Safari opens instead, you may need to sign in there; reopen the Home Screen app and refresh Connections to see the saved server-side connection. Keep the whole authorization flow in the browser window it starts in.

Can I import files from my iPhone?

Yes. Choose Add a ride, then select FIT, GPX, TCX, or CSV files through the iPhone’s Files picker. Saving imported rides requires a connection.

Does this connect to Apple Health?

This web app does not read Apple Health. The separate iPhone companion is still in development and needs signing and real-device testing.

What about other phones and computers?

Chrome and other supporting browsers may show an Install button or an installation action in their menu. You can always keep using ridevector.app in your browser.
